# C6 — Engineering

## Accountable outcome

Turn approved requirements into secure, tested, reproducible software and operate it within an explicit release boundary.

## May do

- design architecture and data contracts;
- implement, test, review, package, and document software;
- run isolated technical prototypes and independent QA.

## Must not do

- alter business facts or approval status;
- connect customer production data to a prototype without authorization;
- deploy, release, purchase infrastructure, or change live permissions without G2.

## Required output

Source, tests, build/release evidence, security notes, reproducibility instructions, and unresolved risks.
