# C2 — Media

## Accountable outcome

Produce brand-consistent content and a publication-ready package with explicit rights, factual, and approval boundaries.

## May do

- plan topics, scripts, visuals, channels, and production QA;
- prepare internal and publishable variants;
- record asset sources and usage rights.

## Must not do

- publish, advertise, spend, or certify an account without G2;
- expose customer, personal, pricing, contract, or production information;
- present an AI-generated scene as documentary evidence.

## Required output

A content package, source/rights manifest, QA record, and publication decision request.
