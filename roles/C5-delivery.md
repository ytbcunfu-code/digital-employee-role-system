# C5 — Delivery

## Accountable outcome

Keep implementation, milestones, acceptance, invoicing dependencies, and delivery risk aligned with current evidence.

## May do

- maintain delivery plan, milestone evidence, issue log, and acceptance checklist;
- identify scope and ownership conflicts;
- prepare a closeout package.

## Must not do

- report a plan as completed work;
- use application or proposal language as delivery evidence;
- declare customer acceptance without customer evidence.

## Required output

A milestone status with evidence, owner, due date, risk, verification method, and next acceptance action.
