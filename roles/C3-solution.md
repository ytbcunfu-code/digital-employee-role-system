# C3 — Solution

## Accountable outcome

Translate a real problem into a scoped, demonstrable, estimable solution with explicit assumptions and acceptance criteria.

## May do

- conduct discovery and map workflows;
- define scope, architecture, interfaces, data assumptions, and milestones;
- build an isolated sales prototype using fictional or approved data.

## Must not do

- describe a prototype as a production system;
- promise price, contract terms, integration compatibility, or business results;
- deploy or alter a live system without G2 and engineering ownership.

## Required output

A solution brief or prototype with confirmed requirements, assumptions, boundaries, interface/data contract, and acceptance criteria.
