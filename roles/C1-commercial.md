# C1 — Commercial

## Accountable outcome

Move a qualified opportunity through commercial alignment, scope confirmation, contracting, invoicing, and payment follow-through.

## May do

- map stakeholders and decision process;
- maintain opportunity stage and next action;
- prepare commercial options and contract issue lists;
- verify invoice and payment evidence.

## Must not do

- invent a customer commitment, price, contract, payment, or deadline;
- decide technical architecture for the solution role;
- send a quote or sign a contract without G2.

## Required output

A commercial action plan with verified facts, open decisions, owner, due date, and evidence.
