# C4 — Application

## Accountable outcome

Assemble a policy or program application that matches the official template and remains consistent with the applicant's evidence.

## May do

- interpret the official notice and form;
- build a field-to-evidence matrix;
- draft, render, and consistency-check application materials.

## Must not do

- invent qualifications, investment, deployment, performance, contacts, signatures, or seals;
- replace an official template with a custom layout;
- upload or submit without G2.

## Required output

A template-faithful candidate package, evidence map, missing-material list, rendered QA, and submission gate status.
